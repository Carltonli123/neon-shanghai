About this build

Streets, building footprints, heights and the river come from OpenStreetMap. Buildings without a height tag get an estimate from their size. The landmarks are hand-built models placed at their real positions.

Graphics: AUTO picks a quality tier for your device and lowers resolution when the frame rate drops. Click it to force Low, Medium, High or Ultra.

Billboards are in-game ad slots. One impression counts when a board is on screen, facing you and at least 18 px tall for 1 continuous second, a simplified version of the MRC display viewability rule. Walls in front of a board are not checked yet.

When the game runs from the Node server, every impression, view time, carjack and wanted level change is logged. See /stats.
